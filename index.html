Market Entry Strategy Proptech

Ontario Property Management Market Entry

PropFlow — Independent Research

Evaluated the viability and go-to-market approach for an AI-native property management platform targeting Ontario's residential rental sector.

Problem

What is the addressable opportunity for an AI-native property management platform in Ontario's residential rental market, and where does the competitive white space lie?

Approach

  • TAM/SAM/SOM sizing for the 100–2,000 unit mid-market
  • Competitive landscape mapping (Yardi, AppFolio, Buildium vs. Canadian incumbents)
  • Regulatory moat analysis — Ontario RTA, PIPEDA compliance burden
  • Workflow-by-workflow automation opportunity mapping

Output

  • Full business overview document
  • Market sizing model
  • Go-to-market sequencing recommendation
  • Course deliverables at Ivey Business School

Key InsightOntario's 100–2,000 unit mid-market is structurally underserved by both enterprise platforms and DIY tools. RTA compliance complexity — typically treated as a cost — functions as a defensible moat when embedded natively in the product layer, making it prohibitively difficult for generalist competitors to replicate.

Operations Product Strategy Sports Tech

Product Roadmap Prioritization Under Resource Constraints

LVLup Sports — Strategy Engagement

Built a structured framework to allocate limited engineering capacity across 12 competing product initiatives for a sports analytics startup.

Problem

How should a capital-constrained sports analytics startup prioritize 12 active product initiatives to maximize strategic value delivered per sprint?

Approach

  • Five-criterion weighted scoring matrix (strategic value, revenue impact, effort, network leverage, retention)
  • Capacity waterfall model mapping available engineering hours to initiative cost
  • Phased three-quarter roadmap with decision gates
  • Capper network segmentation and tier-based engagement strategy

Output

  • Weighted scoring matrix (Excel)
  • Capacity waterfall model
  • Phased roadmap with prioritization rationale
  • Outreach and engagement framework for capper network

Key InsightRevenue-generating features with embedded network leverage were systematically underweighted by the existing roadmap, which optimized for feature completion rather than strategic impact. Reordering by impact-to-effort ratio shifted 40% of capacity toward initiatives with compounding returns on the capper network.

Private Equity ETA Deal Strategy

Search Strategy for Canadian SMB Acquisition

Self-Directed Research

Defined acquisition criteria and search strategy for a self-funded search targeting Canadian SMBs in the $1–5M EBITDA range.

Problem

What acquisition criteria and search strategy maximizes risk-adjusted returns for a first-time acquirer operating without institutional backing in the Canadian lower-middle market?

Approach

  • Industry screening for fragmented markets with recurring revenue and owner-operated dynamics
  • Valuation benchmarking by sector (EV/EBITDA multiples, debt serviceability)
  • Deal sourcing channel analysis — broker networks vs. direct proprietary outreach
  • Risk factor mapping: customer concentration, key-man dependency, margin structure

Output

  • Sector screening framework
  • Acquisition criteria matrix
  • Deal sourcing strategy and outreach playbook
  • Risk-adjusted return analysis by business archetype

Key InsightBlue-collar service businesses with minimal customer concentration, high switching costs, and recurring maintenance contracts offer the best risk-adjusted entry point for self-funded searchers. Proprietary outreach to retiring owner-operators — before they engage a broker — is the primary lever for acquiring at below-market multiples.
